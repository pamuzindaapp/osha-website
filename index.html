Osha Prepayment System

Guaranteed daily payments. Real-time control. Zero excuses.

You hand a driver your car, kombi or motorcycle. They pay through the app before the vehicle starts. Miss a payment and Osha switches the vehicle off remotely.

Can you relate? Owners in Zimbabwe's driver groups post the same stories every week: payments missed for days, phones switched off, trackers removed, vehicles found dumped with no fuel.

    The Osharian family

    No Osharian rides alone

    An Osha is more than a bike. It is a family of riders who look out for each other. When one of us is injured, falls ill, loses a bike or passes on, the rest of us show up with money, with time and with care.

    "When one Osharian falls, we all help carry the load."

    Osharian
    Care Fund    Give · Help · Get help

    Give

    Put a small amount into the Care Fund every month, or give once when a member needs it. Every dollar goes to Osharians and their families.

    Help

    Sign up to help with your time: a lift to hospital, a visit, a hand fixing a bike or running errands for a family.

    Get help

    If something unexpected happens to you or a fellow Osharian, tell us. We check it, then rally the family and the fund around you.

    How the Care Fund works

    1. JoinRegister as an Osharian. Joining is free.
    2. Give what you canContribute monthly or once, by EcoCash, other mobile money or at an Osha store.
    3. Something happensA member is injured, falls ill, loses their bike or passes on. They or a friend ask for help.
    4. Osha checks itWe confirm what happened with the member or their family.
    5. The family respondsMoney goes straight to the member or their family, and volunteers step in where needed.
    6. Everyone sees itMembers get an update on what came in and where it went.

    Our promise to each other

    • Show up for each other, on and off the road
    • Give what you can, when you can. No amount is too small
    • Keep it honest: every request is checked and every dollar is accounted for
    • Respect privacy: we only share what the family agrees to

    The Osharian Care Fund is a voluntary community fund, not insurance. Contributions are gifts, support depends on what the fund holds and is agreed case by case. In an emergency, call 999 or 994 for an ambulance first.
